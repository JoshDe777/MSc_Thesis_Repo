\label{app::recruitment-doc}

\textit{The following email was sent to potential participants, to invite participation to the user study.}\\

Dear [Name],\\
You are invited to take part in a research study exploring the sense of immersion experienced in a virtual reality sports setting, conditional to fulfilling the criteria of eligibility (you must be a legal adult with the full ability to provide consent for your participation, and not part of any population otherwise considered vulnerable, such as e.g. prisoners, asylum-seekers, or diagnosed with a life-limiting or -threatening medical condition).\\
The study involves attending a single session of approximately 60-90 minutes, during which you will play in a prototype VR beach volleyball game, completing a small number of shortened matches with the possibility of adjusting the environment's population to your preference during and between matches. Following this, you will be asked to answer a short questionnaire to assess the experience, particularly the sense that the happenings in the environment are plausible. You may also provide brief written feedback on the overall experience. \\
Participation is entirely voluntary. You do not have to take part, and you may withdraw at any time without penalty, including during the session if you experience any discomfort.\\

If you are interested in taking part, please reply to this email, and you will be provided with more information, including the Participant Information Leaflet to help make up your mind on whether you wish to participate or not.\\

Thank you for considering this invitation, and I will be looking forward to any response.
